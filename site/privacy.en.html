Privacy & important notices

Accounts

The Academy requires an approved account. You can register with an email address, username, and password. Access remains blocked until an administrator has reviewed and approved your account. Supabase processes password hashes, sessions, two-factor authentication factors, and learning progress. If standard Supabase sign-ups are disabled, applications are created through a protected server function. In that case, ownership of the email address is not automatically verified, and administrators are informed of this limitation. Registration is protected against excessive requests using time-limited, pseudonymized request fingerprints.

Profiles, images & friends

You can provide a public profile bio, avatar color, banner design, title, profile picture, and banner image. Uploaded images can be viewed through their public links. Before uploading, photos are exported again as WebP, which removes typical EXIF metadata. Other signed-in Academy members can search for your username. Confirmed friends can see your learning progress only when you have enabled visibility. You can remove friendships.

External videos & links

YouTube videos are embedded only after you click to load them. When opening videos, Discord, or documentation links, the privacy policies of the respective providers apply.

Membership access

Free, Plus, and Ultimate membership access is assigned by administrators. This website does not process payments.

Daily login streaks & achievements

For the optional Daily Streak feature, only explicitly claimed rewards and their dates and times are stored. You must actively press the claim button. After a claim, there is a waiting period of at least 24 hours, and only one claim is permitted per German calendar day (00:00–00:00, Europe/Berlin). If you miss a complete calendar day, your streak returns to 1. Your longest streak is retained as a personal record. Achievement titles are derived from completed lessons, passed exams, friendships, and login days.

Personalization & local storage

Theme, accent colors, font size, layout, background effects, language choice, code drafts, selected lesson parts, and in-progress quiz checkpoints are stored locally in your browser. Resetting the appearance removes related local preferences. These settings and drafts are not automatically transferred to other devices. Completed lessons and awarded XP remain associated with your Supabase account.

Images & file formats

Common formats supported by your browser, including JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, AVIF, or HEIC (depending on the browser), are resized, cropped consistently, and converted into WebP before uploading. Animated images are saved as a still image. SVG is not supported. Uploaded images remain publicly accessible through their storage URLs.

Access & deletion requests

For account deletion and privacy questions, contact nyxatheris in the LiveryLab Discord. Never share passwords or two-factor authentication codes in public channels.

Before a public launch, responsible provider and contact details, a legal notice (Impressum), and legally reviewed privacy information must be added.
